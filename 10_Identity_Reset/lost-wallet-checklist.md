# Lost Wallet Checklist

> DO NOT PUT PLAINTEXT SECRETS HERE. Do not write full card numbers, account numbers, ID numbers, PINs, passwords, recovery codes, or scans in this file. Actual sensitive details belong only inside encrypted vault files.

## First Hour

- Confirm physical safety and retrace the last known location only if safe.
- Lock or replace payment cards through bank/card issuer apps or official phone numbers.
- Disable cards in Apple Pay, Google Wallet, Samsung Wallet, PayPal, Cash App, Venmo, and similar services if the physical card was exposed.
- File a police report if theft is suspected or required for replacement claims.
- Record only issuer name, action taken, case number, and next step in a tracker.

## Same Day

- Banks/cards: request replacement cards, verify mailing address, review recent transactions, and ask about provisional credits for fraud.
- Phone carrier/SIM: if the wallet included phone account PIN paperwork or ID that could support SIM fraud, add/confirm port-out protection.
- Primary email: check for attempted password resets or suspicious security alerts.
- Password manager: do not change everything at once unless compromise is likely; prioritize accounts tied to cards and identity.
- Government IDs: schedule replacement driver's license/state ID. If passport was lost, follow the official passport lost/stolen process.

## Credit Bureaus

- Consider a fraud alert if ID or card data could be misused.
- Consider credit freezes if the wallet included enough identity material to open accounts.
- Use FTC IdentityTheft.gov if fraudulent accounts or identity misuse appear.

## Replacement Tracking

Use `templates/document-replacement-tracker.csv` for:

- Driver's license/state ID.
- Passport or passport card.
- Health insurance card.
- Vehicle insurance card.
- Membership cards.
- Payment cards.

Only store status and reference numbers that are safe to keep in plaintext. Store sensitive scans and full numbers only in encrypted vault files.
