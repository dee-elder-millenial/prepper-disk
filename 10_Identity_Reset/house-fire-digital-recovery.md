# House Fire Digital Recovery

> DO NOT PUT PLAINTEXT SECRETS HERE. Do not store passwords, recovery codes, seed phrases, personal IDs, insurance documents, financial statements, or document scans as plaintext in this normal Prepper Disk tree or dashboard. Actual sensitive material belongs only inside encrypted vault files.

## First Priorities

1. Physical safety, medical care, shelter, pets, and emergency contacts.
2. Fire report number, insurance claim number, photos, and receipts.
3. Email/phone access from a trusted replacement device.
4. Password manager and encrypted vault recovery.
5. Banking, identity documents, cloud/dev accounts, utilities, and insurance portals.

## Documents to Replace or Recover

- Government IDs: driver's license/state ID, passport, Social Security card, birth certificate, marriage/divorce records, vehicle title/registration.
- Financial: bank cards, checkbooks, tax records, insurance policy documents, mortgage/rent documents.
- Medical: insurance card, medication list, provider contacts.
- Household: deed/lease, vehicle documents, inventory photos, warranty records.

Track replacement status in `templates/document-replacement-tracker.csv`. Store scans only inside encrypted vault files.

## Digital Recovery Path

- Primary email: regain access and review security settings before broad password resets.
- Phone carrier/SIM: recover the phone number and protect against unauthorized port-out.
- Password manager: restore from encrypted backup or provider recovery process.
- Google/Apple/Microsoft: restore account access, cloud photos/files, device backups, and MFA.
- Banks/cards: replace cards and check claims-related transactions.
- Cloud/dev: secure GitHub, OpenAI, cloud, registrar, DNS, hosting, and code-signing or deployment access.
- Utilities/insurance: regain insurance, electric, water, internet, gas, rent/mortgage, and vehicle portals.

## References

- Ready.gov financial preparedness for emergency financial document planning.
- FTC IdentityTheft.gov if documents were stolen, misused, or exposed during displacement.

## Vault Reminder

The recommended pattern is encrypted password manager export or KeePassXC `.kdbx`, optional `age`/`gpg` encrypted archives, offline printed recovery envelope, and an offsite encrypted copy. Keep actual vault creation as a future task until Dee chooses the tool.
